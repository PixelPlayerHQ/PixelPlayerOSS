PixelPlayerOSS 0.3.0:

* Offline downloads for Navidrome/Subsonic and Jellyfin, with album downloads and a download manager.
* Twelve translations with an in-app language picker, including Turkish and Simplified Chinese.
* Optional ListenBrainz scrobbling, disabled by default, with custom-server support for Maloja and similar.
* Audio bookmarks, passphrase-protected backups, settings search, and tempo-matched crossfades.
* On-demand MusicBrainz metadata enrichment that never overwrites existing tags.
* Fixes: cloud artwork in notifications, download cancellation races, lyrics rate limiting, shuffle sync.
